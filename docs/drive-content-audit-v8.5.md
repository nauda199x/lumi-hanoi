## Publication safety rules

- Preserve source dates for any commercial term.
- Never publish 2024 price sheets, bank rates or payment schedules as current 2026 terms.
- Do not publish internal contact-person names/phone numbers from bank or booking sheets.
- Do not mix a combined Prestige/Elite amenity list into the wrong phase; assign each named facility only when the source context is clear.
- Keep `NFA/DTSD` and `GFA/DTXD` labels exact; do not merge them.
- For Duplex, sum only matching level-1/level-2 variants.
- Renders are artist impressions, not current-condition or handover evidence.
- Contract/SPA and technical appendices remain the final transaction authority where the source itself says so.
