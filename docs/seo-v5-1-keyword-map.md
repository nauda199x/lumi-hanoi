## Conflict-pair decisions

- **Homepage vs overview:** the homepage remains the brand destination; the overview
  owns project-scale facts. Homepage CTAs explicitly send detail-seeking readers to
  the overview rather than duplicating it.
- **Homepage vs sales/rental:** the homepage may introduce the market but does not
  target inventory, prices, sale or rental terms. Transaction pages own those
  action intents and continue to reject unverified listings.
- **Overview vs phase profiles:** the overview explains the two-phase/nine-tower
  structure; Signature, Prestige and Elite each own a named phase profile.
- **Floor-plan pillar vs unit and phase pages:** the pillar owns the method and
  hierarchy for reading plans. Unit pages own bedroom-format decisions; phase
  pages own phase-specific plan facts.
- **News hub vs articles:** the hub owns browsing/discovery. Each article owns its
  exact procedural or comparison topic and links into the relevant pillar.
- **Sales/rental vs bedroom pages:** sales and rental pages own transaction
  verification. Bedroom pages remain commercial research guides and do not claim
  current stock, price or availability.

No pair is a true duplicate. Therefore no `noindex`, canonical consolidation, URL
change or new public page is recommended. Existing titles, descriptions, H1s,
opening copy, image attributes, Open Graph tags and JSON-LD already align closely
with the mapped intent; the restrained V5.1 implementation changes only ambiguous
homepage CTA anchors and adds broader automated safeguards.
